Difficulty

  • Each faction has its own twist. The enemy picks a different faction every game.
  • Drones fly over rivers and cliffs. Everything else has to find a way around.
  • Drag to move the map. Pinch to zoom. Tap the minimap to jump.
  • Tap a unit to select it. Double-tap grabs every unit of that type on screen. Hold a finger still for 1 second, then drag, to box-select a group.
  • Tap HQ, Barracks or Factory to research upgrades, including your faction's doctrine at HQ.
  • Workers also mine dark oil pools, worth more per trip. Grab the glowing gold relics for an instant ore bonus.
  • Select units, then Save 1/2/3 to make a group. Later, tap Group 1/2/3 to reselect it; tap twice to jump the camera to it.
  • Tap the ground to move. Units fight whatever they meet on the way.
  • Tap your HQ to train workers. They mine ore on their own.
  • Barracks train riflemen and drones. Factories build tanks. Depots add supply.
  • Drones fly and wreck tanks, but tanks can't shoot back. Riflemen and turrets hit both.
  • Engineers (from HQ) repair damaged units and buildings nearby on their own.
  • Urban Network, Shadow Cell and Smugglers can train Hackers once they have a worker and an engineer. Send one to an oil pool to siphon it, or press Hack to drain enemy ore from anywhere.
  • Relics land in a new spot every match. After 8:00, level III weapon and armor upgrades unlock.
